Install and notifications

Install Sandhan like an app. Notices refresh by themselves when the app opens, every 15 minutes while it is open, and when the phone comes back online. Tap the refresh button at the top right to check instantly. Offline, the last copy is shown.

Your data

Saved notices and settings are kept on this device and are never deleted automatically by Sandhan. Only you can clear them. Clearing the browser's site data or uninstalling also removes them, so copy your saved list if you need a backup.

Security and privacy

  • Your name, profile, saved notices and settings are stored only on this device. Nothing personal is sent to any server.
  • Notices come from one read-only list published with the app. Visitors cannot change it.
  • Links open only secure (https) pages. Each notice shows whether its source is an official government or institute site.
  • Sandhan never asks for passwords, payments, Aadhaar or bank details, and has no adverts or tracking. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Fraud warning: real recruiters never charge to guarantee a job or visa. Pay fees only on the official portal named in the advertisement.

About and accuracy

Sandhan gathers recruitment notices from Assam, central government, public sector undertakings, other states, international organisations and research laboratories. Each notice links to the employer's own website and gives its advertisement number where known.

Notices marked Checked with original were read on the employer's or an official site. Notices marked From news reports were summarised from job news sites and should be confirmed on the original before you apply.

Read the original before you apply. Dates and eligibility shown in Sandhan are summaries and can change; the employer's advertisement is final. Sandhan never asks for fees or documents.
